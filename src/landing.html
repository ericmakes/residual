The number is the product

On Trybe your creators get a total. On Residual they get the lines behind it.

How it works

One loop, timed in days, and the clock is public.

You write the brief and set the pay rule. The pool does the rest. The only step that loops back is the compliance gate, and the only deadline is yours: run it to $50 within 14 days of approving it.
