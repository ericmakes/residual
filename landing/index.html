Built for the advertisers Trybe can't serve

Trybe attributes on Shopify sales and runs on Meta. If you sell an app, a subscription, a financial product or you buy media in more than one platform, you were never the customer. You are ours.

01 · attribution

Spend, not sales

Pay rules key on ad-platform spend by ad ID. No pixel, no store sync, no last-click argument. Works for apps, SaaS, fintech, marketplaces and regulated verticals.

02 · channels

Meta, TikTok and Google

Launch an approved video as a Partnership Ad, a Spark Ad or a Demand Gen asset from one screen. One ledger across all three.

03 · compliance

A gate, not a text box

Disclosure line, legal footer, banned claims, required T&Cs: a checklist per market that a video must pass before approval. Reviewers tick, they don't improvise.

04 · creators

Open ledger

Creators see yesterday's spend on each of their videos and what it earned them. Trust is replaced by a number.

05 · creators

Approved means paid

Every approval starts a 14-day run-or-pay clock. If the brand never runs it, the kill fee pays out automatically.

06 · agencies

Many brands, one seat

Programs hold many briefs and pay rules. Agencies manage several brands from one workspace with their own margin on top.
